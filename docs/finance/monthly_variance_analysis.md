## Interpretation discipline

- Revenue variance is favourable when actual is above budget; cost variance is favourable when actual is below budget.
- A zero budget produces a not-meaningful percentage variance rather than division by zero.
- A favourable variance is not automatically sustainable: volume, mix, price, timing and one-off drivers still need investigation.
- This dataset has no balance-sheet, tax, depreciation, financing or cash timing detail; do not label the contribution proxy EBITDA, net income or cash flow.
