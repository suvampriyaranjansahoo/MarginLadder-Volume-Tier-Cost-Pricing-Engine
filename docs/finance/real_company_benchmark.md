Read this as a sensitivity, not a forecast. Bharat Forge carries large export receivables and raw-material and finished-goods stock that the case's quote-automation project would not add. The point is that 45 days is the case input furthest from the real reference, and a reviewer should know how far the answer moves if it is wrong.

## Limits

- Marketplace steel listings are undated and unverified; use them as a rough range only.
- The share price is from 7 May 2026. Do not quote valuation multiples without refreshing it.
- Nothing here changes the case's status: it is still hypothetical, with no users and no interviews.
