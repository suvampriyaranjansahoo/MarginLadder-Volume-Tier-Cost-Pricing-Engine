# 20-minute user call script

Use this with anyone who prices or quotes parts, such as a costing engineer, estimator or purchase executive. Take notes in their words. Do not pitch the tool.

**Open (2 min).** "I am studying how quotes get made. I am not selling anything. Can I ask how the last one went?"

**The last quote (8 min)**
1. Walk me through the last RFQ you quoted, from the email to the quote sent.
2. Where did it wait? For whom? For how long?
3. Which numbers did you type or copy by hand?
4. Who had to approve it? How did you ask? How long did they take?

**Pain (5 min)**
5. What quote did you lose or send late? What happened?
6. What do you check twice because you do not trust it?

**The idea (3 min)** Show the costing screen (`docs/costing_screen.png`).
7. What would you not trust on this screen?
8. What is missing?

**Close (2 min).** "Who else should I talk to?"

## After each call, write down
- Number of days from RFQ to quote on the last job, and the longest wait.
- One quote you could use verbatim.
- One thing that changed your mind.

## How to use the answers
Three calls is enough for one line in the README: "In 3 calls with ..., the longest wait was ... , not costing." Replace Assumption A5 with what you heard.
