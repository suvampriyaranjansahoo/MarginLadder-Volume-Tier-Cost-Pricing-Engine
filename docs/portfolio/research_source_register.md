## Analytical conclusions — judgement, not external facts

1. RFQ response visibility and queue control are plausible operational levers, but the external market reports do not show that a nine-day RFQ cycle is a sector-wide norm.
2. Margin discipline matters in a sector exposed to commodity, energy, logistics and currency volatility. Therefore, recovered volume should be evaluated after contribution and capacity, not treated as revenue-only upside.
3. The Motherson and Bharat Forge disclosures have different revenue definitions, portfolio scope and consolidation characteristics. This release intentionally does not use their headline metrics to assert a peer median, valuation multiple or target margin for the hypothetical SME.
4. The current ICRA FY27 outlook is a forecast. Refresh it before a future interview or publication that depends on current-year conditions.
