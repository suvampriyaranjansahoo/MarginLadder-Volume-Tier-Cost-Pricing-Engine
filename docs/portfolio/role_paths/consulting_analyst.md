# Consulting Analyst path — MarginLadder

## Recruiter takeaway

A structured, answer-first recommendation that treats RFQ turnaround as a hypothesis to investigate, quantifies an assumption-led value pool, compares choices, and stages the investment behind evidence gates.

## 5-minute inspection route

1. Present `docs/consulting/MarginLadder_Executive_Recommendation.pptx`.
2. Read `docs/go_no_go_memo.md` and `docs/consulting/case_deck.md`.
3. Inspect `docs/portfolio/research_source_register.md` and its primary-source URLs.
4. Challenge the recommendation using `docs/finance/model_audit.md` and `docs/finance/capacity_and_queue.md`.

## Demonstrated competencies

- Hypothesis-led issue tree and structured diagnosis.
- Quantitative sizing with assumptions and sensitivity.
- External research with publication date and source limitations.
- Options analysis and recommendation under uncertainty.
- Implementation roadmap, risk controls and decision gates.
- Executive communication in an answer-first deck.

## 60-second walkthrough

“My recommendation is to instrument handoffs and approval queues before funding a broad costing automation build. The case says the cycle is nine working days, but hands-on costing is 12.5 hours, or 1.56 working days. The residual 7.44 days is not yet decomposed, so a technology-first recommendation would be premature. The model estimates an ₹86.4 lakh lost-RFQ margin pool and a ₹21.6 lakh base recovery case, but the 25% win-back is an assumption. I compare a costing screen, a workflow visibility layer and a full integration build, then recommend the reversible option with clear price-parity, capacity and UAT gates. Industry sources are used for context only, not as proof of the case company's economics.”

## Questions to be ready for

- What is your main hypothesis and what evidence would disprove it?
- What is the biggest uncertainty in the value pool?
- Why is workflow visibility a better first option than full automation?
- Why aren't peer company margins a direct benchmark for this SME case?
- What would make you stop the pilot?
- Which decision requires plant capacity confirmation?

## Honest claim boundary

Claim the problem structure, research, options analysis, case modelling and presentation. Do not claim a client recommendation was implemented, accepted by management or delivered measurable benefits.
