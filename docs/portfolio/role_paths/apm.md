# Associate Product Manager path — MarginLadder

## Recruiter takeaway

A focused product bet around RFQ queue visibility and approval control, supported by a PRD, decision log, prioritisation, metrics, pilot guardrails and a task-based discovery/usability protocol. Product-event examples are synthetic and are clearly separated from real user evidence.

## 5-minute inspection route

1. Read `docs/product/PRD_v3.md` and `docs/product/decision_log.md`.
2. Inspect `docs/product/roadmap.md`, `docs/product/prioritisation_matrix.md` and `docs/product/rice_sensitivity.md`.
3. Read `docs/product/discovery_and_usability_pack.md` and `docs/product/pilot_design.md`.

## Demonstrated competencies

- Problem framing and scope discipline.
- Product requirements, non-goals and explicit decision rationale.
- Prioritisation and sensitivity to scoring assumptions.
- Event taxonomy and product metric definitions.
- Experiment/pilot design and kill criteria.
- Human approval and auditability for commercial pricing decisions.

## 60-second walkthrough

“I deliberately did not start with an AI costing product. The product hypothesis is that RFQ owners and estimators need trusted visibility into status, owner, due date, next action and approval state. The PRD defines a narrow workflow visibility layer, while the decision log and roadmap show what is in scope and what is deferred. I defined event properties and funnel metrics, but the current event sample is synthetic and does not show adoption. The discovery and usability pack defines tasks, metrics and critical-control thresholds. No participant sessions or adoption results are claimed in this hypothetical case. I would add document AI only if research proves manual intake is a costly problem and a labelled evaluation set can establish field accuracy and human correction cost.”

## Questions to be ready for

- Who is the primary user and what job are they trying to complete?
- What is the riskiest product hypothesis?
- Why not automate price calculation or approval with AI?
- How would you measure task success and RFQ turnaround?
- Why does synthetic funnel data not establish retention?
- What would make you change the roadmap?

## Honest claim boundary

Claim the PRD, prioritisation, metrics and prototype work. Do not claim user discovery, usability validation, active usage, retention, product-market fit or AI model performance from this case.
